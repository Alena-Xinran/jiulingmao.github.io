Not pet-AI chat. Longitudinal health.

NineLivesCat connects home monitoring with out-of-hospital follow-up. Both products share one engine: capture → score against veterinary scales → compare with this cat’s history → graded next step.

At home

Caught too late

Cats hide pain. Many families notice something is wrong only when it is already late.

At home

Hard to judge

Social feeds and group chats conflict. Mild cases feel expensive; serious cases feel dangerous to wait on.

At home

Broken records

Vets still depend on a parent’s memory. There is rarely a continuous, objective health log.

Clinics have a matching gap: post-op follow-up still lives in a doctor’s WeChat, so high-risk cases get buried.

Capture

Face, stool, vomit, teeth, or a post-op wound — the evidence families can actually record at home.

Quantify

Scores and risk hints grounded in published veterinary scales, not a vague “looks off.”

Compare

Trend against this cat’s own history: slow decline versus a sudden jump — that is what times a visit.

Act

Watch / ask a vet / go in. After surgery: red / yellow / green, so doctors see high-risk first.

When the eye cannot see it yet

Vita, a kitten who has since passed

End-stage kidney failure — too late by the time she reached the hospital. Her family remembered: nothing looked wrong, she just kept looking for water, even when the bowl was full, licking taps in the bathroom and kitchen.

After the mini program launched, they ran a pain check on an earlier photo. The model flagged an abnormal overall state and suggested a clinic workup. Most people would not have seen that in the first picture.

This is a single retrospective case. It is not a diagnosis, and it does not prove the model detected kidney disease. Prospective validation will run in the hospital pilot.

Clinic · 30-second check-ins, high-risk first

Families told us NineLivesCat already helped after surgery — most people do not know how to read a healing wound. We visited Hangzhou clinics and co-designed a follow-up loop so AI helps both the parent and the doctor.

Bind at discharge

Authorize a phone number and pets already on file appear. QR codes work too. No new account to remember.

Daily check-in

Redness, discharge, appetite — all taps, plus one wound photo. About 30 seconds, no typing.

AI first pass

Looks at recent days together and talks about change. Red / yellow / green, with a confidence tag.

Doctor queue

High risk on top, normals collapsed. One-tap canned replies, “come in,” or a manual grade change.

Three colors, because change matters more than today
