About the official NineLivesCat app

NineLivesCat combines AI health scanning with a personal butler chat — ask questions, run checks, and get reports you can share with your vet.

The NineLivesCat app covers five scan types: pain, vomit, stool, dental health, and meow translation. Results are for reference only and do not replace professional veterinary care.
