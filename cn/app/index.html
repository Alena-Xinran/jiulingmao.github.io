A FEW QUESTIONS

Before you get started.

Does NineLivesCat diagnose diseases?

No. NineLivesCat provides risk indicators and health records. A photo cannot establish a diagnosis, and a low-risk assessment cannot rule out a problem. Contact a vet about persistent symptoms.

What is the pain score based on?

Facial indicators reference the Feline Grimace Scale (FGS). NineLivesCat’s 0–18 composite also includes exploratory body indicators. It is not the original FGS, and the expanded score has not been clinically validated.

Does meow translation tell me what my cat is saying?

It uses sound features to suggest possible contexts and states. It cannot translate meows into human language; use it as a way to explore your cat’s behavior.
