HOW FOLLOW-UP WORKS

Clearer updates for owners.
Better records for vets.

  1. Connect the pet at discharge

    Owners authorize their phone number in the mini program to link their pets automatically, with no separate registration or password to remember. Each pet has its own recovery record.

  2. Send a daily update

    Answer simple questions about wound redness, discharge and appetite, then add a wound photo. No typed description is needed for a routine check-in, which takes about 30 seconds.

  3. Review changes and initial flags

    After each check-in, owners receive an initial AI assessment. It compares today’s entry and photos with previous days to flag improvement or deterioration, with a red, amber or green classification for the vet to review.

  4. The vet decides what happens next

    Check-ins appear in the clinic workspace. After reviewing recovery, vets can use a prepared reply, ask the owner to bring their pet in or adjust the classification.

  5. Ask the vet a question

    Owners can write a question and select “Request a vet reply.” It appears in a separate “Awaiting reply” list, where the vet can read the owner’s own words and respond.

THE CLINIC WORKSPACE

What needs your attention today?

AI groups cases by initial status. Vets review the records and decide how to follow up.

Flagged cases appear first

Red cases appear at the top so vets can review possible recovery concerns first. Vets can change the classification after reviewing the records.

Routine cases stay collapsed

Cases with a normal status collapse automatically to keep the list manageable. Vets can still open each case to review daily entries and photos.

Questions have their own queue

A request for a vet reply stays in the awaiting-reply list even when recovery appears normal, so the owner’s question does not disappear into a collapsed group.

A FEW QUESTIONS

Before you get started.

Is this a feature inside the NineLivesCat app?

Jiuling Follow-up is a separate product for clinic-led post-discharge care, with an owner mini program and a clinic workspace. NineLivesCat focuses on everyday health records for cat owners.

Does AI make clinical decisions?

No. AI organizes records and flags possible risks. Vets review cases, adjust classifications and decide on care. Owners should contact their clinic directly in an emergency rather than wait for an online response.

Can our clinic participate?

Contact the team to discuss a pilot. As of August 2026, one Hangzhou veterinary clinic had confirmed participation and was preparing a real-case pilot. Workflow, pilot arrangements and pricing will be discussed with participating clinics.

How do owners get started?

The participating clinic provides instructions at discharge. Owners enter the Jiuling Follow-up mini program and connect their pet; they do not need access to the clinic workspace.
